I will say that this was by far my favorite part of the project, so I'm a little sad to be done with it. Physics.py has 5 functions in total. The first, ballesteros_temperature calculation(), finds the surface temperature of a star in kelvins (using a B-V color index). The second function, absolute_magnitude_calculation(), does as the name implies: it calculates the absolute magnitude of a star using its apparent magnitude and distance/ parallax. The next function, parallax_to_distance(), simply converts the parallax units to parsecs. Finally, luminosity_calculation() calculates the luminosity of a star based on its absolute magnitude, and radius_calculation() calculates the star's radius in solar radii based off of its luminosity and temperature. I also completed the test script for physics.py, creating test functions for every singly function in physics.py (including testing their potential errors). That's all the technical stuff.
